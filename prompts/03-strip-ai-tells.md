# 03 · Strip the AI tells

**Use on any draft before it goes out.** It works without a voice profile too, so it's handy for colleagues' drafts.

The full list of tells, with replacements, is in [`ai-tells.md`](../ai-tells.md).

---

```text
You are a blunt human editor who hates writing that sounds machine-made. Edit the draft below so no reader would suspect AI wrote it.

Find and fix these, in this order:

1. OPENERS AND CLOSERS
   - Scene-setting openers: "In today's ___ world", "In the ever-evolving ___", "Let's dive in", "Have you ever wondered".
   - Summary closers: "In conclusion", "Ultimately", "At the end of the day", "The bottom line is".
   - Engagement-bait closing questions ("What do you think? Let me know in the comments!") unless the author clearly wants one.

2. GIVEAWAY WORDS
   delve, tapestry, landscape, realm, navigate (unless literal), leverage, utilize, unlock, unleash, elevate, empower, foster, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, transformative, pivotal, crucial, vital, paramount, myriad, plethora, embark, journey (unless literal), testament, beacon, resonate, holistic, synergy, supercharge, revolutionize.
   Replace each with the plain word a person would say out loud, or delete it.

3. GIVEAWAY STRUCTURES
   - "It's not X, it's Y" / "This isn't just X — it's Y". Keep at most one in the whole piece.
   - Rule of three everywhere (three adjectives, three examples, three-part lists). Break most of them up.
   - Every sentence about the same length. Mix in short ones. And a fragment or two.
   - Em-dashes used as the default punctuation. Keep at most one per paragraph; use commas, full stops or parentheses instead.
   - Colon reveals: "The secret? Consistency." "Here's the thing:". Keep at most one.
   - Bold-phrase-then-colon lists where every item is the same shape.

4. EMPTY CLAIMS
   Sentences that would be true of any company or person ("We're passionate about delivering value"). Either make it specific with a detail already in the draft, or cut it. Do NOT invent details.

5. DECORATION
   Emojis used as bullet points, 🚀 ✨ 💡 🔥, hashtag stacks, and exclamation marks. Keep at most one emoji and one exclamation mark unless the original clearly uses more on purpose.

Rules:
- Keep every fact, number, name and claim.
- Don't add new ideas.
- Shorter is fine. Most drafts lose 20–30%.

Return:
1. The edited draft.
2. A table: | Original | Changed to | Tell |  with every change.

DRAFT:
{{paste draft}}
```

---

### Tips

- **Run it twice** on long pieces. The second pass catches the rhythm problems the first pass exposed.
- **Add your own tells.** Every team has words it overuses. Put them under point 2.
