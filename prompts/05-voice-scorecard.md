# 05 · Score it before you post

**Use on anything important**: a launch post, a pitch email, your About page. It's a second opinion that checks the draft against *your* profile, not general good writing.

---

```text
You are reviewing a draft for one thing only: does it sound like the person in the voice profile below, and does it sound human?

MY VOICE PROFILE:
{{paste voice profile}}

DRAFT:
{{paste draft}}

Score the draft from 0 to 10 on each of these, with one sentence of evidence for each score (quote the draft):

1. Sounds like me: matches my rhythm, words and stance.
2. Sounds human: no AI giveaway words, structures or decoration.
3. Specific: has concrete details, names, numbers or stories rather than claims anyone could make.
4. Clear: a busy reader gets the point in the first 2 lines.
5. Follows my DO / DON'T rules: list any rule that's broken.

Then:
- OVERALL: the average, and "Post it", "Fix then post" or "Rewrite".
- FIXES: up to 5 lines from the draft that hurt the score most. For each, give the original line and a rewrite in my voice. Don't add new facts.

Be strict. A 10 means my closest friend would assume I wrote it alone.
```

---

### How to read the score

| Overall | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 8–10 | Post it. |
| 6–7 | Apply the fixes, then post. |
| 0–5 | Start again from your notes with [prompt 02A](02-write-in-your-voice.md). Rewriting a weak draft rarely gets past a 7. |
