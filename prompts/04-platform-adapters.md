# 04 · Adapt it per platform

**Use when one idea goes to several places.** Your voice stays the same; the format changes.

---

```text
Turn the piece below into versions for the platforms I list. Keep my voice: same opinions, same words I'd use, same humor level. Only the format changes.

MY VOICE PROFILE:
{{paste voice profile, or delete this section}}

ORIGINAL PIECE:
{{paste the piece}}

PLATFORMS: {{pick from the list below}}

Platform rules:

LINKEDIN
- First 2 lines must make someone click "…see more". Put the most specific, surprising line first. No "I'm excited to announce".
- Short paragraphs (1–2 sentences), line break between them.
- 120–250 words. Max 3 hashtags, at the very end, or none.
- End with a point of view, not a question, unless a question is genuinely the point.

X / THREADS
- Single post: under 280 characters, one idea, no hashtags.
- Thread (only if I ask): first post must stand alone; 4–7 posts; no "🧵" or "1/".

INSTAGRAM CAPTION
- First line works as a hook in the preview (under 125 characters).
- 50–150 words. Up to 5 relevant hashtags at the end.

EMAIL / NEWSLETTER
- Subject line: under 45 characters, specific, no clickbait. Give 3 options.
- Preview text: under 90 characters, adds to the subject instead of repeating it.
- Body: talk to one person. One link, one ask.

SHORT VIDEO SCRIPT (Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, said out loud, under 12 words.
- Written to be spoken: contractions, short sentences, no lists.
- 30–60 seconds (75–150 words). Mark [ON SCREEN: text] where on-screen text helps.

Rules for every version:
- Don't add facts, numbers or stories that aren't in the original.
- Each version should work on its own; don't write "as I said on LinkedIn".

Return each version under a heading with its platform name and its word or character count.
```

---

### Tips

- **Post the native version, not a copy-paste.** The same text everywhere is itself a sign of automation.
- Run each version through [05 · Score it](05-voice-scorecard.md) if it's important.
