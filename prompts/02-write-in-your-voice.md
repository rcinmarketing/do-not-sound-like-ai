# 02 · Write or rewrite in your voice

**Use every time you write.** Paste your voice profile from [prompt 01](01-build-your-voice-profile.md) where it says so, or keep it in a Project / Custom GPT / Gem and skip that part.

---

## A. Write from notes

Start from your own messy notes, not a topic. Notes carry your opinions; a topic alone gets you the internet's average opinion.

```text
Write a {{format, e.g. LinkedIn post / newsletter intro / email to a client}} in my voice.

MY VOICE PROFILE:
{{paste voice profile}}

MY NOTES (messy is fine, these are my real thoughts):
{{paste notes, bullet points, a voice-note transcript}}

AUDIENCE: {{who is reading, and what they already know}}
GOAL: {{what the reader should think, feel or do afterwards}}
LENGTH: {{e.g. under 150 words}}

Rules:
- Use only the ideas, stories and opinions in my notes. Do not add facts, statistics, quotes or examples I didn't give you. If something is missing, put [ASK: what's missing] in the draft instead of inventing it.
- Follow every DO / DON'T rule in my voice profile.
- Match the rhythm of my signature examples, not their topic.
- No opening line about "today's world", trends or the landscape. Start with the most specific thing in my notes.
- No closing question to the reader unless my notes include one.

Give me ONE draft. Then, under it, list any place where you weren't sure what I'd say.
```

---

## B. Rewrite an existing draft

For something written by AI, by a colleague, or by you on a tired day.

```text
Rewrite the draft below so it sounds like me.

MY VOICE PROFILE:
{{paste voice profile}}

DRAFT:
{{paste draft}}

Rules:
- Keep every fact, number, name and claim exactly as it is. Change how it's said, not what is said.
- Cut anything that doesn't earn its place. My version can be shorter.
- Follow every DO / DON'T rule in my voice profile.
- Don't add new ideas.

Return:
1. The rewrite.
2. A short list of the 5 biggest changes and why each one makes it sound more like me.
```

---

### Tips

- **Give it a real story.** "Write about burnout" gets you generic advice. "Write about the week I said yes to four launches and cried in the parking lot" gets you a post.
- **The [ASK] markers are a feature.** They're where the model would otherwise have made something up.
- Run the result through [03 · Strip the AI tells](03-strip-ai-tells.md) before posting.
