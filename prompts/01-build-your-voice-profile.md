# 01 · Build your voice profile

**Use once.** Gives you a voice profile to reuse in every chat after this.

**You need:** 3–5 pieces of your own writing (about 800+ words in total). Pick ones you're proud of and that weren't heavily edited by someone else or by AI. Mixing types helps: a post, an email, a talk transcript, a voice note you transcribed.

---

```text
You are a linguist and editor. Your job is to describe how one specific person writes, so precisely that another writer could imitate them and a close friend wouldn't notice.

Below are samples of my writing. Analyze ONLY what is actually in them. Do not describe what good writing in general looks like, and do not flatter me.

Produce a VOICE PROFILE with these sections:

1. ONE-LINE SUMMARY
   How I sound, in one sentence a stranger could picture. Use a comparison if helpful ("like a ___ explaining ___ to ___").

2. RHYTHM
   - Typical sentence length, and how much it varies. Quote my shortest and longest sentence.
   - Paragraph length.
   - How I open a piece (quote 2 openings).
   - How I end a piece (quote 2 endings).

3. WORD CHOICE
   - Words and phrases I use often (with counts if you can).
   - Words I would never use, judging by register. Be specific.
   - Do I use contractions, slang, profanity, jargon, numbers, emojis? How often?

4. POINT OF VIEW AND STANCE
   - I / we / you: which do I use, and how?
   - How confident do I sound? Do I hedge? Quote an example.
   - How do I handle disagreement or opinions?

5. HUMOR AND PERSONALITY
   Describe it with quoted examples, or say "none observed".

6. FORMATTING HABITS
   Lists, bold, line breaks, headings, hashtags, punctuation quirks (dashes, ellipses, parentheses, exclamation marks).

7. DO / DON'T RULES
   10 rules another writer must follow to sound like me. Each rule must be checkable, e.g. "Never more than one exclamation mark per post", not "Be authentic".

8. SIGNATURE EXAMPLES
   Copy 3 short passages (1–3 sentences each) from my samples that are the most "me". These will be used as examples, so pick well.

Keep the whole profile under 600 words. Write it in plain markdown so I can save it and paste it into future chats.

MY WRITING SAMPLES:

--- SAMPLE 1 ---
{{paste sample}}

--- SAMPLE 2 ---
{{paste sample}}

--- SAMPLE 3 ---
{{paste sample}}
```

---

### Tips

- **Read the profile and correct it.** If it says "uses humor often" and you don't, delete that. You know your voice better than the model does.
- **Redo it every few months**, or when your writing changes (new job, new audience).
- **Have more than one voice?** Make a profile for each one, e.g. *LinkedIn me* and *client email me*.
