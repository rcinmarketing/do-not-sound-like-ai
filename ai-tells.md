# AI tells: what gives AI writing away, and what to say instead

None of these words is banned in itself. The problem is density: five of them in one post and readers stop trusting it. Use this list with [prompt 03](prompts/03-strip-ai-tells.md), or add it to your voice profile's DON'T rules.

## Strict writing constraints

Sound Like Me and the Claude skill apply these to every draft:

1. **No rhetorical pivots.** Absolutely no "It's not X, it's Y" phrasing. No rhetorical questions used as dramatic transitions ("If X happens, what survives?").
2. **Narrative congruence.** A continuous, logical flow. No abrupt switches from long, complex sentences to one-word or short-fragment "punchlines".
3. **Natural sentence structure.** No symmetrical paragraph pattern (long sentence, short sentence, 3 bullets, repeat). Let sentences run long when they connect ideas naturally.
4. **No hype.** If you wouldn't say it face to face over coffee, don't write it.

## Phrases

| Tell | Why it reads as AI | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "In today's fast-paced world…" | Scene-setting nobody needed | Start with your first specific sentence |
| "Let's dive in!" | Announces instead of doing | Just start |
| "Here's the thing:" / "The secret? ___." | Fake suspense | Say the thing |
| "In conclusion" / "At the end of the day" | Essay-template closer | End on your strongest line |
| "I hope this helps!" / "Feel free to reach out" | Assistant voice | A real ask, or nothing |
| "What do you think? Let me know below! 👇" | Engagement bait | Ask only a question you genuinely want answered |
| "Great question!" | Assistant voice | Answer it |

## Structures (the bigger giveaway)

| Pattern | Example | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Contrast flip | "It's not about working harder — it's about working smarter." | At most one per piece |
| Rule of three | "fast, flexible, and future-proof" | Use one or two, or four. Real lists are uneven. |
| Same-length sentences | Every sentence 15–20 words | Add a short one. Like this. |
| Em-dash everywhere | "This tool — built for teams — changes everything — fast." | Commas, full stops, parentheses |
| Bold-label lists | **Speed:** … **Scale:** … **Simplicity:** … | Write a paragraph, or vary the items |
| Hedging stack | "This can potentially help to somewhat improve…" | Commit, or say what you don't know |
| Empty universal claims | "Customer experience matters more than ever." | A detail only you could write |

## Decoration

- Emojis as bullet points (✅ 🚀 💡 🔥 ✨)
- Hashtag stacks (#Marketing #AI #Growth #Leadership #Innovation)
- More than one exclamation mark
- Title Case In Every Heading When You Normally Wouldn't

## The test

Read it out loud. If you'd never say a sentence to a colleague across the table, rewrite it until you would.
